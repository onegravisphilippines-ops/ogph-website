Who we are

United by the road.
Connected as one.

One Gravis Philippines (OGPH) is a motorcycle riding community established in 2021. We value the people behind the helmets as much as the journeys we share.

Our community is grounded in brotherhood and camaraderie, responsible riding, mutual respect and meaningful participation in group activities and the wider community.

Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Does submitting an application make me an Official Member?

No. Submission begins the application process. The application will be reviewed and membership is subject to the group's current evaluation and approval process.

What happens after I submit my application?

An authorized membership officer should review the information and contact you about orientation, familiarization and any next steps.

Who can access my personal information?

Membership information should be accessible only to authorized OGPH officers who need it to process and manage membership. The live website must use secure access controls and an approved privacy notice.

Where can I see OGPH updates?

Follow the official One Gravis Philippines Facebook page for announcements and community updates.
